# Website Feature Ideas for a Village Carpenter & Fabricator

Since most visitors will use budget smartphones on slow internet, keep the site simple, fast, and photo-heavy.

## Must-Have Basics

- **Big "Call Now" button:** One tap to call your father directly. This is the most important feature.
- **WhatsApp button:** Lets people send photos and chat easily.
- **Home page introduction:** His photo, name, years of experience (e.g., "25 years of experience"), and a one-line description of his work.
- **Services list with photos:** Doors, windows, beds, almirahs, door frames, temples (mandir), farm tools, trolleys, roof work, repairs. Each service is shown in both wood and aluminium options where available.
- **Work gallery:** Real photos of past work. Villagers trust what they can see.

## New Features

- **Before and After photos:** Side-by-side images (or a slider) showing the transformation of real work, such as:
  - Old broken door → new door (wood or aluminium)
  - Empty room → fitted almirah
  - Bare window opening → finished window
  - Old, damaged furniture → repaired or polished piece
  - Home or shop before and after the full work
  - Tip: Take the "before" photo from the same angle as the "after" photo so the difference is easy to see.
- **Customer Map with Photo Pins:**
  - A map page showing a pin for each village or customer location the work was done at.
  - Each pin shows a small photo of the finished work (or of the customer's house or work).
  - Tapping a pin plays the customer's short feedback video, where they talk about the work.
  - Tapping a pin can also show the village name, type of work (e.g., "Aluminium window"), and a "Call for similar work" button.
  - This builds strong trust and shows how many villages he serves.
  - Tips:
    - Ask customers for their permission before showing their video or house location.
    - Mark the pin at the village or approximate area rather than the exact house, to protect privacy.
    - Keep each video short (30-60 seconds) so it plays on slow internet.

## Trust-Building Features

- **Customer reviews:** Short feedback with the village name, e.g., "Suresh ji from Rampur: made a great door."
- **"Villages we serve" list:** Names of all 5-15 villages so people feel it's local.
- **Short video:** A 1-2 minute clip of your father talking about his work or working.
- **Simple guarantees:** Strong material, on-time delivery, repair warranty.

## Customer Convenience

- **Approximate price guide:** "Doors starting from ₹X" so customers don't hesitate to ask.
- **Quote by photo:** Send a photo on WhatsApp to get a rough estimate.
- **Free home measurement:** "We visit your home to take measurements."

## Village-Friendly Touches

- **Less text, more photos:** Short sentences, large letters.
- **Audio message:** A 30-second "Namaste, I am ... speaking" in your father's voice, helpful for people who can't read well.
- **QR code:** Print it on pamphlets, the shop, trolley, or vehicle so people can open the site instantly.
- **Google Maps / Business Profile:** So "carpenter near me" searches show his name.
- **Big buttons and clear colors:** Easy to tap with a finger.

## Future Additions

- **Order status updates:** WhatsApp message like "Your door is ready."
- **Festival/wedding season offers:** e.g., bed and almirah sets for weddings.
- **Referral reward:** "Recommend us and get a discount."
