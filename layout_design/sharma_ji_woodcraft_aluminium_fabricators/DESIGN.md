## Brand & Style
The design system reflects the intersection of generational timber artisanship and modern industrial metal fabrication. It speaks directly to homeowners, village community members, regional contractors, and local builders who demand durability, unyielding honesty, and proven master craftsmanship.

The visual language balances **Tactile Craftsmanship** with **High-Contrast Utilitarian Clarity**. The aesthetic avoids fragile tech-startup minimalism in favor of grounded, physical credibility: deep teakwood tones, aged amber varnish highlights, milled aluminium chamfers, and clear structural lines. Surfaces feel rooted, tangible, and sturdy, echoing physical workshops with heavy planed lumber, polished extrusion channels, and brass rivets. Interface elements are purposefully direct, ensuring effortless operation for semi-urban and rural users across varied device capabilities.

## Colors
The palette pairs raw organic timber warmth with machined architectural aluminium:

- **Primary (`#6F3E1E`)**: Teakwood Brown. Anchors core branding, primary headers, structural containers, and master trust badges.
- **Secondary (`#D97706`)**: Polished Honey Amber. Applied to pricing highlights, warranty guarantees, star ratings, and craftsmanship callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#475569`)**: Extruded Aluminium Slate. Represents precision fabrication, metal frames, sliding track specs, and structural dividers.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FDFBF7`)**: Warm Crisp Ivory. Delivers high contrast for effortless legibility under bright outdoor sun while eliminating the harsh coldness of sterile pure white.

### Specialized Utility Colors
- **WhatsApp Action (`#15803D`)**: Deep vibrant emerald green reserved exclusively for direct chat, catalog sharing, and measurement submissions.
- **Instant Call Action (`#DC2626` / `#EA580C`)**: High-visibility vermilion-crimson to orange gradient for immediate voice contact and emergency repairs.
- **Surface Elevation Tone (`#F4EFE6`)**: Natural unplaned timber grain wash for card containers and comparison track backgrounds.

## Typography
Typography is tuned for instant bilingual legibility across Devanagari (Hindi) and Latin (English) scripts. 

- **Display & Headlines (`Epilogue`)**: Architectural, chiseled, and confident. Heavy weights (700 and 800) evoke woodcut block lettering and heavy workshop stamp marks.
- **Body & Functional Labels (`Work Sans`)**: Open aperture, balanced grotesque proportions that remain razor-sharp on low-cost IPS mobile screens and bright sunlight conditions.

Text hierarchies emphasize readability: critical rates per square foot, wood species (Sheesham, Sagwan, Teak), gauge thicknesses (16G, 18G aluminium), and contact details must never fall below 14px.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-column structural grid system built to handle real-world inspection galleries and fabrication listings:
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 16px margins and gutters. Single-column stacked inspection sheets, oversized thumb-friendly action bars fixed to viewport bottom.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout with 24px margins and gutters. Two-column service matrices.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout with max-width container capped at 1280px, anchored by 40px outer margins.

Spacing follows an unambiguous 8-pt modular grid scale. Spacing tokens explicitly determine structural breathing room between service portfolios, dimension tables, customer location markers, and dual-action floating communication anchors.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth mimics physical shop fittings and material overlap rather than sterile digital drop-shadows:
- **Baseline Surface**: Warm ivory `#FDFBF7`.
- **Card Containers**: `#FFFFFF` with a 1.5px bevel-cut stroke in `#E2D9CC` (warm clay outline) paired with an ambient shadow: `0px 4px 12px rgba(111, 62, 30, 0.06)`.
- **Raised Interactive Units**: Buttons, price tags, and village badges carry a dual-tone physical extrusion: a crisp bottom border rim (`2px solid rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)`) giving each control a tangible, pressable, shop-tool quality.
- **Floating Operational Deck**: The mobile bottom contact dock floats above content using a deep amber-tinted ambient shadow (`0px -4px 20px rgba(71, 85, 105, 0.15)`) coupled with a solid 1px top border in `#CBD5E1`.

## Shapes
Corner geometry uses a controlled radius level of **2 (Rounded)**. 
- Standard surfaces, panels, input fields, and material cards use an 8px (`0.5rem`) corner radius, reminiscent of hand-sanded timber edges and deburred aluminium section profiles.
- Elevated highlight cards use 16px (`1rem`).
- Micro-indicators, status chips, and price per-square-foot tags leverage full pill curvature to distinguish numerical data and material status from orthogonal card containers.

## Components

### 1. Dual-Core Action Buttons
- **Direct WhatsApp Order Button**: Solid background `#15803D` with crisp `#FFFFFF` typography and standard chat icon. Minimum tap height 52px. Features a tactile 2px extruded bottom edge in `#166534`.
- **Direct Phone Hotline Button**: Solid background `#DC2626` with 2px bottom rim `#991B1B`. Clear click-to-call icon accompanied by bold bilingual subtext ("Call Sharma Ji / बात करें").

### 2. Before-and-After Interactive Sliders
A specialized showcase component for structural renovations (e.g., rusted iron gate replaced with architectural wood-finish aluminium):
- Two overlapping full-bleed images split by a draggable 4px aluminium track (`#475569`).
- Center grab handle: 44px round milled brass disc (`#D97706`) with double white directional arrows.
- Floating contrast labels ("Before / पहले" vs "After / बाद में") positioned top-left and top-right in semi-translucent slate chips.

### 3. Price & Dimension Pill Tags
- Compact tags showing rates (e.g., "₹280/sq.ft", "₹450/sq.ft with installation").
- Styled with a warm amber wash background (`#FEF3C7`), teakwood text (`#6F3E1E`), and a 1px border (`#FDE68A`). Pill radius: 9999px.

### 4. Village & Locality Customer Trust Pins
- Chips identifying past regional installations (e.g., "Installed in Rampur Village • 2 Days Ago").
- Slate grey background (`#F1F5F9`) with tertiary text (`#475569`) and a miniature rust-orange location marker pin.

### 5. Material & Gauge Selection Chips

- Inactive state: `#FFFFFF` surface with `#E2D9CC` border.
- Active state: `#6F3E1E` background with `#FFFFFF` text and a brass-gold checkmark.

### 6. Craftsmanship Guarantee Badges
